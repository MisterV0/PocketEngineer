Battery Science

Energy Fundamentals

Battery capacity is measured in Watt-hours (Wh), representing the total energy stored. When a device draws power (measured in Watts), the battery depletes at a predictable rate. This calculator assumes a standard 100Wh battery capacity.

%

Battery Level (State of Charge)

Percentage of total capacity remaining. A 50% charge on a 100Wh battery means 50Wh of usable energy is available.

W

Power Consumption

The rate at which the device draws energy, measured in Watts. Higher power consumption depletes the battery faster.

t

Time Remaining

Calculated using: Time (hours) = Available Energy (Wh) ÷ Power Draw (W)

Real-World Notes

  • • Battery efficiency varies with temperature and discharge rate
  • • This calculator assumes constant power draw
  • • Real devices may shut down before battery reaches 0%
  • • Battery health degrades over time, reducing capacity
